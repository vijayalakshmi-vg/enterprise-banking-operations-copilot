FAQ 1: Failed or Pending ATM Cash Withdrawal

If an ATM does not dispense cash but your account is charged, or your cash withdrawal remains pending, the transaction may need time to process or reverse. Allow up to three business days for processing. If the amount has not been credited or reversed after this period, contact the bank's support team with the transaction date and reference number.

FAQ 2: Card Declined

If a debit card transaction is declined, check the available account balance, card status, daily transaction limits, and any applicable security restrictions. If the problem continues, contact bank support.

FAQ 3: Lost or Stolen Card

If a debit card is lost or stolen, immediately use the bank's official card-locking service or contact its support team to block the card. Review recent transactions and report any unauthorized activity.

FAQ 4: International Transaction Fees

International card transactions may incur foreign transaction fees or currency conversion charges depending on the bank's terms and the card product. Check the applicable fee schedule for the specific card.

FAQ 5: Unrecognized or Suspicious Cash Withdrawal

If you notice a suspicious, unfamiliar, or unrecognized cash withdrawal on your bank statement, and you do not remember making or authorizing it, treat it as a potentially unauthorized transaction. Report the suspicious withdrawal to the bank immediately. Provide the transaction date, amount, and reference number, and follow the bank's official dispute process.
