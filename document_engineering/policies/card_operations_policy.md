## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the operational procedures for issuing, activating, managing, replacing and securing customer debit cards.

It establishes requirements for card activation, PIN management, lost or stolen card reporting, replacement requests, delivery timelines and transaction restrictions.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to individual customers holding eligible personal banking debit cards.

It covers:
- Card issuance and activation.
- PIN creation and changes.
- Lost or stolen card reporting.
- Damaged card replacement.
- Card delivery and tracking.
- Card transaction restrictions.
- Card-related escalation procedures.

Credit card operations are outside the scope of this demonstration policy.

## 3. Card Issuance

### 3.1 Eligibility

Customers with eligible active personal banking accounts may request a debit card.

Card issuance is subject to account eligibility and identity verification.

**Rule ID:** CARD-ISS-001

### 3.2 Card Issuance Confirmation

Once a card request is approved, the card issuance system must record the request reference and issuance status.

Customers should receive a confirmation notification after successful issuance.

**Rule ID:** CARD-ISS-002

## 4. Card Activation

### 4.1 Activation Eligibility

Customers must complete the approved identity verification procedure before activating a newly issued debit card.

**Rule ID:** CARD-ACT-001

### 4.2 Activation Methods

For this synthetic policy, card activation may be completed through an approved mobile banking application or authorized customer support channel.

**Rule ID:** CARD-ACT-002

### 4.3 Failed Activation

If card activation fails repeatedly, the customer must be referred to the Card Operations team for investigation.

The card must remain inactive until successful activation is confirmed.

**Rule ID:** CARD-ACT-003

## 5. PIN Management

### 5.1 PIN Creation

Customers must establish a PIN through an approved secure banking channel.

The PIN must not be shared with bank employees or other individuals.

**Rule ID:** CARD-PIN-001

### 5.2 PIN Change

Customers may request a PIN change through an approved authentication procedure.

Identity verification is mandatory before completing the change.

**Rule ID:** CARD-PIN-002

### 5.3 Incorrect PIN Attempts

For this synthetic policy, five consecutive incorrect PIN attempts trigger a temporary card security restriction.

The customer must complete the approved verification procedure before the restriction can be reviewed.

**Rule ID:** CARD-PIN-003

## 6. Lost or Stolen Cards

### 6.1 Reporting Requirements

Customers must report lost or stolen cards immediately through an approved banking channel.

The report must include the customer reference and available card identification information.

Customers must never be asked to disclose their complete PIN or password.

**Rule ID:** CARD-LOSS-001

### 6.2 Card Blocking

After a verified lost or stolen card report, the card must be blocked to prevent further authorized transactions.

**Rule ID:** CARD-LOSS-002

### 6.3 Fraud Investigation

If unauthorized transactions are reported, the case must be referred to the Account Security and Fraud Operations team.

The customer must receive a case reference.

**Rule ID:** CARD-LOSS-003

## 7. Card Replacement

### 7.1 Replacement Eligibility

Customers may request a replacement card when the existing card is:

- Lost.
- Stolen.
- Physically damaged.
- Expired.

Replacement requests are subject to identity verification.

**Rule ID:** CARD-REP-001

### 7.2 Replacement Processing

For this synthetic policy, an approved replacement request must be processed within one business day.

This timeline refers to processing the request, not physical delivery.

**Rule ID:** CARD-REP-002

### 7.3 Replacement Exceptions

Replacement requests involving suspected fraud or unresolved account restrictions must be reviewed by the appropriate authorized team.

**Rule ID:** CARD-REP-003

## 8. Card Delivery and Tracking

### 8.1 Delivery Timeline

For this synthetic policy, standard card delivery is expected within five to seven business days after dispatch.

Delivery timelines may vary depending on the delivery location and courier operations.

**Rule ID:** CARD-DEL-001

### 8.2 Delayed Delivery

Customers who have not received a card within the expected delivery period may request a delivery investigation.

The request should include the card order reference and dispatch date.

**Rule ID:** CARD-DEL-002

### 8.3 Incorrect Delivery Address

If a card is returned because of an incorrect delivery address, the customer must update and verify their registered address before another dispatch is arranged.

**Rule ID:** CARD-DEL-003

## 9. Card Transaction Restrictions

### 9.1 Security Restrictions

Cards may be temporarily restricted when suspicious transaction activity is identified.

The restriction must be reviewed by the authorized security team.

**Rule ID:** CARD-SEC-001

### 9.2 International Transactions

International transaction access is subject to the customer's card settings and applicable security controls.

**Rule ID:** CARD-SEC-002

### 9.3 Restriction Removal

A card restriction must not be removed until the required security verification and authorization are completed.

**Rule ID:** CARD-SEC-003

## 10. General Restrictions

1. Employees must never request a customer's complete PIN or password.
2. Lost or stolen cards must be blocked after verified reporting.
3. Replacement cards must not be dispatched to an unverified delivery address.
4. Card restrictions must only be removed by authorized personnel.
5. Card issuance, activation and replacement activities must be recorded for audit purposes.

**Rule ID:** CARD-GEN-001

## 11. Escalation Procedure

Cases must be referred to the Card Operations team when:

- Card activation repeatedly fails.
- Replacement requests remain unresolved.
- Card delivery exceeds the expected timeline.
- Card restrictions cannot be resolved through standard procedures.

Suspected unauthorized transactions must be referred to the Account Security and Fraud Operations team.

Every escalation must include the case reference, reason for escalation and investigation outcome.

**Rule ID:** CARD-ESC-001

## 12. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Debit card | A payment card linked to an eligible bank account |
| Card activation | Process of enabling a newly issued card |
| PIN | Personal Identification Number used for card authentication |
| Card blocking | Restricting further use of a card |
| Replacement card | A new card issued to replace an existing card |
| Dispatch date | Date on which the card is handed over for delivery |
| Business day | A working day excluding weekends and designated bank holidays |
