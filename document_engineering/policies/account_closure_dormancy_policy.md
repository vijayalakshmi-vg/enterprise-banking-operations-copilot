# Account Closure and Dormancy Policy

**Document Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the procedures for identifying inactive and dormant accounts, processing customer-requested account closures, managing outstanding balances and handling account reactivation requests.

It defines account status classifications, closure eligibility, verification requirements, processing timelines, restrictions and escalation procedures.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to individual personal banking accounts.

It covers:

- Inactive accounts.
- Dormant accounts.
- Customer-requested account closure.
- Account closure eligibility.
- Outstanding balances.
- Pending transactions.
- Account reactivation.
- Closure confirmation.
- Exceptions and escalation.

Business accounts, deceased customer accounts and accounts subject to legal proceedings require separate specialist procedures.

## 3. Account Status Classification

### 3.1 Active Account

An active account is an account that is open and available for eligible banking operations.

An account may remain active even if the customer has not recently performed a transaction.

**Rule ID:** ACD-STATUS-001

### 3.2 Inactive Account

For this synthetic policy, an account may be classified as inactive after six consecutive months without a customer-initiated financial transaction.

An inactive account remains open unless a separate restriction or closure action applies.

**Rule ID:** ACD-STATUS-002

### 3.3 Dormant Account

For this synthetic policy, an account may be classified as dormant after 12 consecutive months without a customer-initiated financial transaction.

Dormant accounts may have additional transaction restrictions until the required verification and reactivation procedures are completed.

**Rule ID:** ACD-STATUS-003

### 3.4 Restricted Account

A restricted account is an account subject to a limitation imposed by an authorized banking or compliance process.

Restrictions may result from security concerns, suspected fraud, legal requirements or verification issues.

An inactive or dormant classification does not automatically mean that an account is restricted.

**Rule ID:** ACD-STATUS-004

### 3.5 Closed Account

A closed account is an account for which the authorized closure process has been completed and the account is no longer available for normal transactions.

A closure request alone does not mean the account is already closed.

**Rule ID:** ACD-STATUS-005

## 4. Inactive and Dormant Account Management

### 4.1 Inactivity Monitoring

Eligible personal accounts must be reviewed for inactivity according to the defined monitoring process.

The monitoring process must distinguish customer-initiated transactions from system-generated activities.

**Rule ID:** ACD-MON-001

### 4.2 Dormancy Classification

Before classifying an account as dormant, the account servicing team must verify the last qualifying customer-initiated financial transaction date.

System-generated interest credits, scheduled maintenance entries and bank-initiated adjustments must not automatically reset the inactivity period under this synthetic policy.

**Rule ID:** ACD-MON-002

### 4.3 Customer Notification

For this synthetic policy, customers must receive a notification at least 30 calendar days before an eligible account is classified as dormant.

The notification should explain the expected status change and the available customer action.

**Rule ID:** ACD-MON-003

### 4.4 Dormant Account Restrictions

A dormant account may have restrictions on selected transactions until the customer's identity is verified and the account is reactivated.

Incoming payments and other account activities must be handled according to the applicable account controls.

**Rule ID:** ACD-MON-004

## 5. Account Reactivation

### 5.1 Reactivation Request

A customer may request reactivation of an eligible inactive or dormant account.

The request must be registered and linked to the relevant account reference.

**Rule ID:** ACD-REACT-001

### 5.2 Identity Verification

The customer must complete the required identity verification before reactivation is approved.

Verification must follow the applicable customer information and account security procedures.

**Rule ID:** ACD-REACT-002

### 5.3 Reactivation Approval

Reactivation may proceed only after the verification requirements are satisfied and no unresolved restriction prevents account access.

An account subject to a legal or fraud-related restriction must not be reactivated through the ordinary dormancy process.

**Rule ID:** ACD-REACT-003

### 5.4 Reactivation Timeline

For this synthetic policy, an eligible reactivation request must be reviewed within two business days after all required verification information is received.

Additional investigation may extend the processing time.

**Rule ID:** ACD-REACT-004

## 6. Customer-Requested Account Closure

### 6.1 Closure Eligibility

A customer may request account closure when:

- The customer has completed the required identity verification.
- The account is eligible for closure.
- Outstanding obligations have been reviewed.
- No unresolved restriction prevents closure.
- Pending transactions have been assessed.

**Rule ID:** ACD-CLOSE-001

### 6.2 Closure Request Registration

Every closure request must receive a unique service reference.

The request record must include:

- Customer account reference.
- Closure request date.
- Reason for closure, if provided.
- Current account status.
- Outstanding balance.
- Pending transaction details.
- Verification status.

**Rule ID:** ACD-CLOSE-002

### 6.3 Identity Verification

The customer must complete the required identity verification before account closure is approved.

Employees must not bypass verification because the customer has previously accessed the account.

**Rule ID:** ACD-CLOSE-003

### 6.4 Pending Transactions

Before closure, the account servicing team must review pending transactions, unsettled payments, scheduled payments and unresolved disputes.

Where a pending transaction prevents closure, the customer must be informed of the issue and the next required action.

**Rule ID:** ACD-CLOSE-004

### 6.5 Outstanding Obligations

Outstanding fees, loan-related obligations, negative balances and other applicable liabilities must be reviewed before closure.

An account must not be represented as fully closed while an unresolved obligation prevents completion.

**Rule ID:** ACD-CLOSE-005

## 7. Balance Settlement

### 7.1 Positive Balance

Where an account has a positive available balance, the customer must be provided with an eligible settlement method according to the applicable banking procedures.

The destination account or payment method must satisfy the required ownership and verification checks.

**Rule ID:** ACD-BAL-001

### 7.2 Negative Balance

An account with a negative balance must be reviewed for outstanding obligations.

The customer must be informed of the amount due and the required settlement process.

**Rule ID:** ACD-BAL-002

### 7.3 Pending Credits

Pending incoming payments must be reviewed before final closure.

The handling of pending credits must follow the applicable transaction processing rules.

**Rule ID:** ACD-BAL-003

### 7.4 Balance Confirmation

The final balance must be verified before closure is completed.

The account servicing team must retain evidence of the settlement or other approved balance disposition.

**Rule ID:** ACD-BAL-004

## 8. Account Closure Confirmation

### 8.1 Closure Approval

Account closure must be completed only after the required verification, transaction review and balance settlement checks are satisfied.

**Rule ID:** ACD-CONF-001

### 8.2 Closure Status

The account closure process must use an appropriate status:

- Closure requested.
- Verification pending.
- Pending transactions under review.
- Outstanding balance pending.
- Closure approved.
- Closure completed.
- Closure rejected.
- Closure on hold.

**Rule ID:** ACD-CONF-002

### 8.3 Customer Confirmation

Once closure is completed, the customer must receive confirmation that identifies the closed account and the effective closure date.

**Rule ID:** ACD-CONF-003

### 8.4 Closed Account Restrictions

A closed account must not be treated as an active account for new customer-initiated transactions.

Any subsequent financial activity must be handled under the applicable banking procedures.

**Rule ID:** ACD-CONF-004

## 9. Closure Processing Exceptions

A closure request must be placed on hold or escalated when:

- The account has an unresolved negative balance.
- Pending transactions remain unsettled.
- A dispute or investigation is ongoing.
- Identity verification is incomplete.
- A legal or regulatory restriction applies.
- The account is subject to a fraud-related restriction.
- Ownership information is inconsistent.
- An outstanding loan or other obligation requires specialist review.

**Rule ID:** ACD-EXC-001

## 10. Processing Timelines

The following are synthetic demonstration targets:

| Activity | Target Processing Time |
|---|---|
| Closure request acknowledgement | One business day |
| Initial closure eligibility review | Two business days |
| Dormant account reactivation review | Two business days after verification |
| Standard account closure | Three business days after all requirements are satisfied |
| Closure confirmation | One business day after closure completion |

Cases involving pending transactions, legal restrictions, outstanding balances or additional verification may take longer.

These targets are not guaranteed completion times.

**Rule ID:** ACD-TIME-001

## 11. Exceptions and Escalation

Cases must be escalated to the Account Services Manager or appropriate specialist team when:

- Account ownership cannot be verified.
- The account is subject to a legal restriction.
- Suspected fraud is identified.
- Outstanding obligations remain unresolved.
- A pending dispute affects the closure request.
- Account status information is inconsistent.
- A closure request remains unresolved beyond its expected timeline.

The escalation record must include the account reference, closure request reference, current status, reason for escalation and actions already taken.

**Rule ID:** ACD-ESC-001

## 12. General Controls

1. Account status must be verified before any servicing action.
2. Inactive, dormant, restricted and closed statuses must remain distinct.
3. Customer identity verification is mandatory for eligible reactivation and closure requests.
4. Pending transactions and outstanding balances must be reviewed before closure.
5. Closure approval must be recorded.
6. Customers must receive confirmation after successful closure.
7. Legal and fraud-related restrictions must follow specialist procedures.
8. AI assistants must not independently reactivate or close customer accounts.

**Rule ID:** ACD-GEN-001

## 13. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Active account | Account available for eligible banking operations |
| Inactive account | Account with no qualifying customer-initiated financial transaction for the defined inactivity period |
| Dormant account | Account classified as dormant after the defined inactivity period |
| Restricted account | Account subject to an authorized operational or legal limitation |
| Closed account | Account whose authorized closure process is complete |
| Reactivation | Process of restoring eligible account activity |
| Balance settlement | Process of resolving and disposing of the remaining account balance |
| Pending transaction | Transaction that has not reached its final processing status |
