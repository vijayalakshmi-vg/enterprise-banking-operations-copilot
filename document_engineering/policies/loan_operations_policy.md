# Loan Operations Policy

**Document Owner:** Lending Operations Manager

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the procedures for processing personal loan applications, assessing eligibility, reviewing applications, approving loans, disbursing funds, managing repayments and handling overdue payments.

It establishes operational requirements for the personal lending lifecycle.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to individuals applying for personal loans through the synthetic banking platform.

It covers:

- Loan application submission.
- Customer eligibility.
- Required documentation.
- Credit assessment.
- Loan approval and rejection.
- Loan disbursement.
- Repayment management.
- Overdue payments.
- Loan closure and exceptions.

Business loans, mortgage loans and secured commercial lending are outside the scope of this demonstration policy.

## 3. Loan Application

### 3.1 Application Eligibility

Applicants must:

1. Be at least 18 years old.
2. Hold an eligible personal banking profile.
3. Provide complete application information.
4. Complete the required identity verification procedures.
5. Meet the applicable synthetic lending assessment criteria.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-APP-001

### 3.2 Application Information

Applicants must provide:

- Full legal name.
- Date of birth.
- Residential address.
- Employment information.
- Monthly income.
- Existing financial obligations.
- Requested loan amount.
- Preferred repayment period.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-APP-002

### 3.3 Application Reference

Every submitted application must receive a unique loan application reference.

The application reference must be used for status tracking and subsequent investigation.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-APP-003

### 3.4 Incomplete Applications

Applications containing missing mandatory information must remain pending.

The applicant must be notified about the information required to continue processing.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-APP-004

## 4. Loan Eligibility and Assessment

### 4.1 Income Assessment

The applicant's declared income must be reviewed as part of the lending assessment.

Supporting income documentation may be requested when additional verification is required.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-ELIG-001

### 4.2 Existing Financial Obligations

Existing financial obligations must be considered when assessing the applicant's repayment capacity.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-ELIG-002

### 4.3 Credit Assessment

The lending assessment must consider available credit information, repayment capacity and applicable internal lending criteria.

A credit assessment result alone must not automatically guarantee loan approval.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-ELIG-003

### 4.4 Additional Assessment

Applications with inconsistent financial information or unresolved eligibility concerns must be referred for additional review.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-ELIG-004

## 5. Required Documentation

### 5.1 Identity Documents

Applicants must provide valid identity documentation accepted under the Account Opening and KYC Policy.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-DOC-001

### 5.2 Income Documents

For this synthetic policy, accepted income documents include:

- Recent salary statements.
- Employer-issued income certificates.
- Approved income verification records.

The lending team may request additional documents when submitted information is incomplete.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-DOC-002

### 5.3 Document Validation

Submitted documents must be reviewed for completeness, consistency and validity.

Unclear or inconsistent documents must be referred for manual verification.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-DOC-003

## 6. Loan Approval and Rejection

### 6.1 Approval Requirements

A loan may be approved when:

1. The applicant meets the eligibility criteria.
2. Required documentation is complete.
3. Identity verification is successful.
4. The lending assessment is completed.
5. Required authorization is obtained.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-APPROVE-001

### 6.2 Approval Authority

Loan approval must be performed by authorized lending personnel or an approved lending decision system operating within defined authorization limits.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-APPROVE-002

### 6.3 Application Rejection

An application may be rejected when mandatory eligibility requirements are not met or the lending assessment does not support approval.

The decision and reason must be recorded.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-APPROVE-003

### 6.4 Pending Approval

Applications awaiting additional documents, assessment or authorization must remain pending.

Pending applications must not be treated as approved loans.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-APPROVE-004

## 7. Loan Disbursement

### 7.1 Disbursement Eligibility

Loan funds may be disbursed only after final approval and completion of mandatory pre-disbursement requirements.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-DISB-001

### 7.2 Disbursement Processing

For this synthetic policy, approved loan funds must be transferred to the verified customer account within two business days after all disbursement requirements are satisfied.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-DISB-002

### 7.3 Disbursement Failure

If loan disbursement fails, the transaction must be investigated by Lending Operations.

The loan must not be marked as successfully disbursed until the transfer is confirmed.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-DISB-003

## 8. Loan Repayment

### 8.1 Repayment Schedule

An approved loan must have a repayment schedule specifying:

- Outstanding principal.
- Applicable interest.
- Repayment amount.
- Payment due dates.
- Remaining repayment period.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-REP-001

### 8.2 Scheduled Payments

Customers must make repayments according to the agreed repayment schedule.

Payment records must be updated after successful payment confirmation.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-REP-002

### 8.3 Early Repayment

Customers may request early repayment of an outstanding loan.

Any applicable conditions, charges or adjustments must be reviewed under the Fees and Charges Policy.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-REP-003

### 8.4 Repayment Failure

If a scheduled repayment fails, the payment status must be recorded and the customer must receive an appropriate notification.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-REP-004

## 9. Overdue Payments

### 9.1 Overdue Identification

A repayment is classified as overdue when the scheduled due date has passed without confirmed payment.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-OVERDUE-001

### 9.2 Customer Notification

Customers with overdue repayments should receive a notification containing the outstanding repayment information and available next steps.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-OVERDUE-002

### 9.3 Overdue Investigation

If a customer reports that a repayment was completed but the loan account still shows an overdue status, Lending Operations must investigate the payment reference and transaction records.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-OVERDUE-003

### 9.4 Financial Hardship

Customers reporting financial hardship must be referred to the appropriate authorized lending support team for review.

The AI assistant must not independently approve changes to repayment terms.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-OVERDUE-004

## 10. Loan Closure

### 10.1 Closure Eligibility

A loan may be considered for closure when all outstanding repayment obligations have been settled and the final payment has been confirmed.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-CLOSE-001

### 10.2 Closure Confirmation

After successful closure, the loan account status must be updated and a closure confirmation should be provided to the customer.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-CLOSE-002

### 10.3 Outstanding Balances

Loans with outstanding principal, interest or applicable charges must not be marked as fully settled.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-CLOSE-003

## 11. Processing Timelines

The following are synthetic demonstration processing targets:

| Activity | Target Processing Time |
|---|---|
| Initial application review | Two business days |
| Additional document review | Two business days |
| Lending assessment | Three business days |
| Final approval processing | One business day |
| Approved loan disbursement | Two business days |

These are demonstration targets and are not guaranteed completion times.

Applications requiring additional investigation may take longer.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-TIME-001

## 12. Exceptions and Escalation

Applications must be escalated to the Lending Operations Manager when:

- Financial information is inconsistent.
- Mandatory documents cannot be verified.
- Credit assessment requires additional authorization.
- Loan disbursement remains unresolved.
- A repayment is disputed.
- An overdue payment remains unresolved.
- A customer requests a repayment arrangement due to financial hardship.

Each escalation must include the loan reference, reason for escalation, supporting records and investigation outcome.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-ESC-001

## 13. General Controls

1. Loan approvals must follow authorized lending procedures.
2. Employees must not bypass mandatory eligibility or documentation requirements.
3. Loan disbursements must be recorded and independently traceable.
4. Customer financial information must only be accessed by authorized personnel.
5. Repayment records must be maintained for audit purposes.
6. Loan status changes must have an auditable record.
7. AI assistants must not independently approve loans, modify repayment schedules or waive outstanding obligations.

**Rule ID:** LOAN-GEN-001

## 14. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Applicant | Individual requesting a personal loan |
| Principal | Original loan amount excluding interest and other charges |
| Interest | Cost charged for borrowing funds |
| Disbursement | Transfer of approved loan funds to the customer |
| Repayment schedule | Agreed plan specifying payment amounts and due dates |
| Overdue payment | Scheduled repayment not confirmed by its due date |
| Outstanding balance | Remaining amount owed on a loan |
| Loan closure | Formal completion of a loan after settlement of obligations |
