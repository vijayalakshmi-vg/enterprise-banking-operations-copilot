## 1. Purpose

This policy defines procedures for identifying suspicious account activity, reporting suspected fraud, applying account security restrictions, investigating unauthorized transactions and escalating security incidents.

Its objective is to protect customer accounts and ensure that security incidents are handled consistently.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to individual customers holding personal banking accounts.

It covers:

- Suspicious account activity.
- Unauthorized transactions.
- Account security restrictions.
- Fraud reporting.
- Identity verification during investigations.
- Security incident investigation.
- Escalation and resolution procedures.

Card-specific blocking and replacement procedures are additionally governed by the Card Operations Policy.

## 3. Suspicious Account Activity

### 3.1 Identification

An account may be flagged for review when unusual activity is identified by the bank's monitoring system.

Examples include:

- Multiple transactions inconsistent with established account activity.
- Unusual transaction locations.
- Repeated unsuccessful authentication attempts.
- Unexpected changes to registered customer information.
- Transaction patterns identified by security monitoring controls.

A flagged transaction does not automatically establish that fraud has occurred.

**Rule ID:** SEC-SUS-001

### 3.2 Initial Review

The Account Security team must review flagged activity and determine whether additional investigation is required.

The review must consider transaction details, account history and available security information.

**Rule ID:** SEC-SUS-002

### 3.3 Customer Notification

Where appropriate and permitted by security procedures, customers must be notified about suspicious activity affecting their accounts.

Notifications must not disclose sensitive security monitoring information.

**Rule ID:** SEC-SUS-003

## 4. Unauthorized Transaction Reporting

### 4.1 Customer Reporting

Customers who identify transactions they do not recognize must report them through an approved banking channel.

The report should include:

- Transaction reference number.
- Transaction date.
- Transaction amount.
- Transaction description.
- Reason the transaction is disputed.

Customers must not disclose their full passwords, PINs or one-time authentication codes when reporting suspected fraud.

**Rule ID:** SEC-UNAUTH-001

### 4.2 Case Registration

Every unauthorized transaction report must receive a unique investigation reference.

The case record must include the customer's report, transaction details, submission date and initial investigation status.

**Rule ID:** SEC-UNAUTH-002

### 4.3 Initial Assessment

The Fraud Operations team must assess the report and determine whether immediate protective action is required.

The existence of a customer complaint alone does not establish that a transaction was fraudulent.

**Rule ID:** SEC-UNAUTH-003

## 5. Account Security Restrictions

### 5.1 Restriction Eligibility

The bank may apply a temporary account restriction when credible security concerns require further investigation.

Examples include:

- Suspected unauthorized access.
- Unresolved identity verification concerns.
- Confirmed security incidents requiring containment.
- Suspicious account activity requiring authorized review.

**Rule ID:** SEC-REST-001

### 5.2 Restriction Types

For this synthetic policy, security restrictions may include:

1. Temporary restriction on selected transactions.
2. Restriction on changes to customer information.
3. Temporary restriction on digital banking access.
4. Restriction on outgoing transfers.

The specific restriction must be proportionate to the identified security concern and authorized under the applicable procedure.

**Rule ID:** SEC-REST-002

### 5.3 Customer Impact

When an account restriction is applied, the customer should receive an appropriate notification where permitted.

The notification should explain the next steps without revealing confidential security information.

**Rule ID:** SEC-REST-003

## 6. Identity Verification During Security Investigations

### 6.1 Verification Requirements

Customers requesting assistance with security-related account issues must complete the approved identity verification procedure.

**Rule ID:** SEC-IDV-001

### 6.2 Failed Verification

If identity verification fails, sensitive account information must not be disclosed.

The case must be referred for an alternative verification procedure when appropriate.

**Rule ID:** SEC-IDV-002

### 6.3 Verification Restrictions

Employees must not bypass identity verification because a customer claims urgency or familiarity with the bank.

**Rule ID:** SEC-IDV-003

## 7. Fraud Investigation

### 7.1 Investigation Initiation

A formal investigation must be initiated when the initial assessment identifies reasonable grounds for further review.

**Rule ID:** SEC-INV-001

### 7.2 Evidence Collection

The investigation team may review:

- Transaction references and timestamps.
- Authentication records.
- Available account activity.
- Customer-submitted evidence.
- Relevant security alerts.
- Related case history.

Access to investigation records must be limited to authorized personnel.

**Rule ID:** SEC-INV-002

### 7.3 Investigation Outcome

The investigation must record one of the following outcomes:

- Confirmed unauthorized activity.
- No evidence of unauthorized activity.
- Inconclusive investigation.
- Further information required.

The outcome must be supported by documented evidence.

**Rule ID:** SEC-INV-003

## 8. Restriction Removal

### 8.1 Eligibility for Removal

An account restriction may be considered for removal after the underlying security concern has been reviewed and resolved.

**Rule ID:** SEC-REMOVE-001

### 8.2 Authorization

Only authorized security personnel may approve the removal of a security restriction.

An AI assistant or automated chatbot must not independently remove account restrictions.

**Rule ID:** SEC-REMOVE-002

### 8.3 Unresolved Cases

Restrictions must remain in place when the required investigation or authorization is incomplete.

Customers should be directed to the appropriate authorized support channel.

**Rule ID:** SEC-REMOVE-003

## 9. Escalation Procedure

Cases must be escalated to the Fraud Operations Manager when:

- Multiple suspicious transactions are identified.
- Unauthorized access is suspected.
- Customer identity cannot be verified.
- An investigation remains inconclusive.
- A security restriction cannot be resolved through standard procedures.
- There is evidence of a coordinated fraud incident.

All escalations must include the investigation reference, reason for escalation, supporting evidence and current case status.

**Rule ID:** SEC-ESC-001

## 10. General Security Controls

1. Customer passwords, PINs and authentication codes must never be requested or stored in investigation notes.
2. Security restrictions must only be applied or removed by authorized personnel or approved systems.
3. Investigation records must be protected against unauthorized access.
4. Security-related customer information must only be disclosed after appropriate verification.
5. All security decisions must have an auditable record.
6. Suspected security incidents must be handled according to approved escalation procedures.

**Rule ID:** SEC-GEN-001

## 11. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Suspicious activity | Account activity that requires additional security review |
| Unauthorized transaction | A transaction reported or identified as potentially not authorized by the customer |
| Account restriction | A limitation applied to selected account operations |
| Fraud investigation | A formal review of suspected fraudulent activity |
| Security incident | An event that may compromise account or customer information security |
| Investigation reference | A unique identifier assigned to a security case |
| Authorized personnel | Employees or systems permitted to perform specified security operations |
