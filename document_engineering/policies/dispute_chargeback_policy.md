## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the procedures for receiving, classifying, investigating and resolving customer disputes involving banking transactions and card payments.

It establishes requirements for dispute registration, supporting evidence, investigation timelines, chargeback processing, customer communication and escalation.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to individual customers who report concerns involving:

- Unauthorized card transactions.
- Duplicate transactions.
- Incorrect transaction amounts.
- Goods or services not received.
- Refunds not received.
- Cash withdrawals not received.
- Other eligible transaction disputes.

Loan repayment disputes and general service complaints must be handled under their respective policies unless they directly involve a disputed transaction.

## 3. Dispute Classification

### 3.1 Unauthorized Transactions

An unauthorized transaction is a transaction the customer states they did not initiate or approve.

Examples include:

- Card transactions the customer does not recognize.
- Transactions reported after card details were compromised.
- Suspicious online payments.

**Rule ID:** DIS-UNAUTH-001

### 3.2 Duplicate Transactions

A duplicate transaction occurs when the same payment appears to have been processed more than once for a single purchase or service.

The investigation must compare transaction references, amounts, dates and merchant information.

**Rule ID:** DIS-DUP-001

### 3.3 Incorrect Transaction Amount

A customer may dispute a transaction when the charged amount differs from the amount authorized or agreed upon.

The investigation must review the original authorization and final posted transaction.

**Rule ID:** DIS-AMT-001

### 3.4 Goods or Services Not Received

A customer may dispute a card payment when the purchased goods or services were not delivered as agreed.

The customer may be asked to provide order confirmations, merchant communications or delivery information.

**Rule ID:** DIS-GOODS-001

### 3.5 Refund Not Received

A refund dispute may be registered when a merchant confirms that a refund was initiated but the customer cannot identify the corresponding credit.

The investigation must request the merchant refund reference or other supporting evidence when available.

**Rule ID:** DIS-REFUND-001

### 3.6 Cash Withdrawal Not Received

A customer may dispute an ATM withdrawal when the account is debited but the requested cash is not dispensed or is only partially dispensed.

The investigation must review the ATM transaction record and available terminal information.

**Rule ID:** DIS-ATM-001

## 4. Dispute Registration

### 4.1 Required Information

A dispute request should contain:

- Customer account reference.
- Transaction reference.
- Transaction date.
- Transaction amount and currency.
- Merchant or ATM information, where applicable.
- Dispute category.
- Description of the issue.

**Rule ID:** DIS-REG-001

### 4.2 Initial Acknowledgement

For this synthetic policy, the customer must receive an acknowledgement within one business day after a complete dispute request is registered.

The acknowledgement must include a dispute reference number.

**Rule ID:** DIS-REG-002

### 4.3 Incomplete Requests

If required information is missing, the dispute team must request the additional details needed to proceed.

The request must remain marked as awaiting customer information until the required details are received.

**Rule ID:** DIS-REG-003

## 5. Supporting Evidence

### 5.1 Evidence for Unauthorized Transactions

Depending on the case, the investigation may require:

- Customer confirmation that the transaction was not authorized.
- Transaction details.
- Relevant card security information.
- Confirmation of when the customer noticed the activity.

Customers must never be asked to disclose their PIN, password or one-time authentication code.

**Rule ID:** DIS-EVID-001

### 5.2 Evidence for Duplicate Transactions

The investigation should compare:

- Original transaction reference.
- Suspected duplicate reference.
- Transaction amounts.
- Merchant details.
- Posting dates.

**Rule ID:** DIS-EVID-002

### 5.3 Evidence for Goods or Services Not Received

Relevant evidence may include:

- Order confirmation.
- Delivery status.
- Merchant correspondence.
- Cancellation confirmation, where applicable.

**Rule ID:** DIS-EVID-003

### 5.4 Evidence for ATM Disputes

Relevant evidence may include:

- ATM identifier.
- Withdrawal date and time.
- Amount requested.
- Amount dispensed, if known.
- ATM transaction reference.

**Rule ID:** DIS-EVID-004

## 6. Investigation Procedures

### 6.1 Transaction Verification

The dispute team must verify that the disputed transaction exists and matches the customer's reported details.

**Rule ID:** DIS-INV-001

### 6.2 Transaction Status Review

The investigation must determine whether the transaction is:

- Pending.
- Posted.
- Reversed.
- Refunded.
- Declined.

A pending authorization must not automatically be treated as a completed transaction.

**Rule ID:** DIS-INV-002

### 6.3 Merchant Investigation

Where applicable, the dispute team must review merchant information and request supporting transaction evidence.

**Rule ID:** DIS-INV-003

### 6.4 Investigation Record

The investigation record must include:

- Dispute reference.
- Transaction reference.
- Dispute category.
- Evidence received.
- Actions performed.
- Investigation findings.
- Final outcome.

**Rule ID:** DIS-INV-004

## 7. Chargeback Management

### 7.1 Chargeback Eligibility

A chargeback may be initiated when the transaction meets the applicable card network rules and the dispute investigation supports proceeding.

A customer complaint alone does not guarantee chargeback eligibility.

**Rule ID:** DIS-CB-001

### 7.2 Chargeback Submission

Before submission, the dispute team must verify:

- Applicable dispute reason.
- Required supporting evidence.
- Submission deadline.
- Transaction eligibility.
- Applicable card network requirements.

**Rule ID:** DIS-CB-002

### 7.3 Chargeback Status

Chargebacks must be tracked using an appropriate status:

- Under review.
- Evidence pending.
- Submitted.
- Merchant response received.
- Accepted.
- Rejected.
- Closed.

**Rule ID:** DIS-CB-003

### 7.4 Chargeback Outcome

The final outcome must be communicated to the customer with an explanation consistent with the investigation findings.

A chargeback must not be represented as successful until the applicable process confirms the outcome.

**Rule ID:** DIS-CB-004

## 8. Provisional Credits

### 8.1 Eligibility Review

A provisional credit may be considered where permitted by applicable rules and authorized internal procedures.

Eligibility must be reviewed before any temporary credit is issued.

**Rule ID:** DIS-CREDIT-001

### 8.2 Provisional Credit Restrictions

A provisional credit is temporary and must not be communicated as a final dispute resolution.

The credit may be adjusted or reversed according to the final investigation outcome and applicable requirements.

**Rule ID:** DIS-CREDIT-002

## 9. Dispute Resolution Outcomes

Each completed investigation must be assigned one of the following outcomes:

1. Dispute accepted.
2. Dispute rejected with documented reason.
3. Transaction reversed.
4. Refund confirmed.
5. Chargeback initiated.
6. Additional information required.
7. Case escalated.

The resolution record must include the reason for the outcome and the supporting evidence.

**Rule ID:** DIS-RES-001

## 10. Processing Timelines

The following are synthetic demonstration processing targets:

| Activity | Target Processing Time |
|---|---|
| Initial dispute acknowledgement | One business day |
| Initial transaction review | Two business days |
| Duplicate transaction investigation | Three business days |
| ATM dispute investigation | Five business days |
| Initial investigation update | Five business days |
| Customer notification after final decision | Two business days |

Chargeback processing timelines may depend on card network rules, merchant responses and external dependencies.

These targets are not guaranteed resolution times.

**Rule ID:** DIS-TIME-001

## 11. Exceptions and Escalation

Cases must be escalated to the Dispute Resolution Manager when:

- A customer reports suspected fraud.
- The transaction evidence is inconsistent.
- A chargeback deadline is approaching.
- Required merchant evidence is unavailable.
- The investigation exceeds its expected processing timeline.
- A customer challenges the final investigation outcome.
- The case involves multiple related transactions.

Suspected fraud must also be handled under the Account Security and Fraud Policy.

**Rule ID:** DIS-ESC-001

## 12. General Controls

1. Every dispute must receive a unique reference number.
2. Transaction status must be verified before classifying a payment as completed.
3. Supporting evidence must be retained with the investigation record.
4. Chargeback eligibility must be checked against applicable rules.
5. Provisional credits require authorization.
6. Final outcomes must be documented and communicated.
7. Customers must never be asked to share passwords, PINs or one-time authentication codes.
8. AI assistants must not independently submit chargebacks, issue credits or make final dispute decisions.

**Rule ID:** DIS-GEN-001

## 13. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Transaction dispute | Customer challenge concerning a financial transaction |
| Chargeback | Card payment reversal process governed by applicable card network rules |
| Provisional credit | Temporary credit issued during an eligible investigation |
| Merchant | Business that accepts payment for goods or services |
| Authorization | Approval associated with a payment request |
| Posted transaction | Transaction recorded as completed in the account |
| Dispute reference | Unique identifier assigned to a dispute case |
