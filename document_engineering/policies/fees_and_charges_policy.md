# Fees and Charges Policy

**Document ID:** POL-FEE-007  
**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Department:** Banking Operations and Finance  
**Document Owner:** Banking Finance Manager  
**Status:** Synthetic Demonstration Policy  
**Effective Date:** 01 November 2026  
**Review Status:** Not an official banking policy

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the procedures for assessing, applying, reviewing and refunding fees and service charges associated with personal banking operations.

It establishes fee categories, transaction charges, waiver eligibility, refund conditions, disputed fee investigation and customer notification requirements.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to individual customers holding eligible personal banking accounts.

It covers:

- Account maintenance fees.
- External transfer charges.
- Card replacement charges.
- Loan-related charges.
- Fee waivers.
- Fee refunds.
- Disputed charges.
- Fee notification and escalation.

Business banking fees and commercial pricing arrangements are outside the scope of this demonstration policy.

## 3. Fee Categories

### 3.1 Account Maintenance Fees

For this synthetic policy, an eligible personal account may incur a monthly maintenance fee of $5.

The fee must be applied according to the account's applicable fee schedule.

**Rule ID:** FEE-ACCT-001

### 3.2 External Transfer Fees

For this synthetic policy, an external bank transfer may incur a processing fee of $2 per successfully processed transfer.

The fee must not be applied to a transaction that has been rejected before successful processing.

**Rule ID:** FEE-TRANS-001

### 3.3 Card Replacement Fees

For this synthetic policy, a replacement debit card requested because of loss or physical damage may incur a $10 replacement fee.

A replacement requested because of a confirmed manufacturing defect is eligible for a fee waiver.

**Rule ID:** FEE-CARD-001

### 3.4 Loan Processing Fees

For this synthetic policy, an approved personal loan may incur a processing fee of $25.

The fee must be disclosed to the applicant before final loan acceptance.

**Rule ID:** FEE-LOAN-001

## 4. Fee Application Rules

### 4.1 Customer Notification

Customers must be informed of applicable fees before completing a chargeable service request.

The notification must identify the fee category and applicable amount.

**Rule ID:** FEE-APPLY-001

### 4.2 Fee Calculation

Fees must be calculated according to the applicable fee schedule.

Employees must not manually alter fee amounts without authorized approval.

**Rule ID:** FEE-APPLY-002

### 4.3 Duplicate Charges

If the same fee is applied more than once for a single eligible service event, the duplicate charge must be investigated.

Confirmed duplicate charges must be corrected through the approved adjustment procedure.

**Rule ID:** FEE-APPLY-003

## 5. Fee Waivers

### 5.1 Waiver Eligibility

A fee waiver may be considered when:

- A service failure is attributable to the bank.
- A replacement card is issued because of a confirmed manufacturing defect.
- An authorized customer service adjustment is approved.
- A documented operational error results in an incorrect fee.

**Rule ID:** FEE-WAIVER-001

### 5.2 Waiver Authorization

Fee waivers must be approved by personnel with the appropriate authorization.

The reason, amount and approving authority must be recorded.

**Rule ID:** FEE-WAIVER-002

### 5.3 Waiver Restrictions

Customers must not be promised a fee waiver before eligibility and authorization are confirmed.

An AI assistant must not independently approve or apply fee waivers.

**Rule ID:** FEE-WAIVER-003

## 6. Fee Refunds

### 6.1 Refund Eligibility

A customer may request a fee refund when a charge appears incorrect, duplicated or inconsistent with the applicable fee schedule.

**Rule ID:** FEE-REFUND-001

### 6.2 Refund Investigation

The investigation must review:

- Original transaction reference.
- Fee transaction reference.
- Fee category.
- Date of charge.
- Applicable fee schedule.
- Previous adjustments or refunds.

**Rule ID:** FEE-REFUND-002

### 6.3 Refund Approval

A refund may be processed only after the investigation confirms that the charge was incorrect or that an authorized exception applies.

**Rule ID:** FEE-REFUND-003

### 6.4 Refund Processing Timeline

For this synthetic policy, an approved fee refund must be processed within three business days after final authorization.

This timeline does not guarantee when the customer's available balance will reflect the adjustment.

**Rule ID:** FEE-REFUND-004

## 7. Disputed Fees

### 7.1 Customer Dispute

Customers may dispute a fee they believe was incorrectly applied.

The dispute should include:

- Customer account reference.
- Fee transaction reference.
- Fee amount.
- Date of charge.
- Reason for the dispute.

**Rule ID:** FEE-DISPUTE-001

### 7.2 Dispute Classification

Fee disputes must be classified as one of the following:

- Incorrect fee amount.
- Duplicate fee.
- Unauthorized fee.
- Fee applied to an ineligible transaction.
- Fee waiver not reflected.
- Other fee-related concern.

**Rule ID:** FEE-DISPUTE-002

### 7.3 Investigation Outcome

The investigation must record one of the following outcomes:

- Fee correctly applied.
- Fee incorrectly applied.
- Duplicate charge confirmed.
- Additional information required.
- Escalation required.

**Rule ID:** FEE-DISPUTE-003

## 8. Fee Schedule Updates

### 8.1 Update Authorization

Changes to fee amounts or fee categories must be approved by the authorized Finance and Banking Operations representatives.

**Rule ID:** FEE-UPDATE-001

### 8.2 Effective Date

Every approved fee schedule change must include an effective date.

Charges must be assessed using the fee schedule applicable to the transaction or service date.

**Rule ID:** FEE-UPDATE-002

### 8.3 Historical Records

Previous fee schedules must be retained for audit and dispute investigation.

The current fee schedule must be clearly distinguishable from superseded versions.

**Rule ID:** FEE-UPDATE-003

## 9. General Controls

1. All fees must follow the approved fee schedule.
2. Customers must receive appropriate fee disclosures.
3. Duplicate and incorrect charges must be investigated.
4. Fee waivers require authorized approval.
5. Refunds must be supported by documented investigation outcomes.
6. Historical fee records must remain available for audit.
7. AI assistants must not independently apply, waive or refund fees.

**Rule ID:** FEE-GEN-001

## 10. Processing Timelines

The following are synthetic demonstration processing targets:

| Activity | Target Processing Time |
|---|---|
| Initial fee dispute review | Two business days |
| Duplicate fee investigation | Two business days |
| Fee waiver review | One business day |
| Approved fee refund processing | Three business days |
| Fee schedule update after approval | One business day |

These are demonstration targets and are not guaranteed completion times.

Requests requiring additional investigation may take longer.

**Rule ID:** FEE-TIME-001

## 11. Exceptions and Escalation

Cases must be escalated to the Banking Finance Manager when:

- A fee is inconsistent with the applicable schedule.
- Multiple duplicate charges are identified.
- A refund remains unresolved.
- A waiver request exceeds the employee's authorization limit.
- Historical fee information cannot be verified.
- A customer disputes an investigation outcome.

Each escalation must include the fee reference, amount, reason for escalation and investigation history.

**Rule ID:** FEE-ESC-001

## 12. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Service fee | Charge applied for an eligible banking service |
| Fee schedule | Approved list of applicable banking fees |
| Fee waiver | Authorized decision not to apply an otherwise applicable fee |
| Fee refund | Return of a previously collected fee |
| Duplicate charge | Repeated application of the same fee for one service event |
| Effective date | Date from which an approved fee schedule becomes applicable |
| Superseded version | A previous version replaced by a newer approved version |
