## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the procedures for receiving, registering, classifying, investigating, resolving and escalating customer complaints.

It defines complaint handling responsibilities, acknowledgement requirements, resolution targets, customer communication standards and recordkeeping controls.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to complaints submitted by individual banking customers regarding:

- Poor customer service.
- Delayed service requests.
- Account servicing problems.
- Incorrect or incomplete information.
- Communication failures.
- Unresolved service requests.
- Repeated operational errors.
- Dissatisfaction with a previous complaint outcome.

Transaction disputes, fraud reports and fee disputes must also follow their respective specialist policies.

## 3. Complaint Classification

### 3.1 Service Quality Complaints

A service quality complaint concerns dissatisfaction with the quality, accuracy or professionalism of a banking service.

Examples include:

- Unprofessional employee conduct.
- Incorrect information provided by support staff.
- Repeated requests for the same information.
- Poor communication during service delivery.

**Rule ID:** COM-CLASS-001

### 3.2 Service Delay Complaints

A service delay complaint occurs when a customer reports that a service request has not been completed within its expected processing timeline.

Examples include:

- Delayed address updates.
- Delayed card replacement.
- Delayed account servicing requests.
- Lack of progress updates.

The investigation must verify the original request date, applicable processing target and current request status.

**Rule ID:** COM-CLASS-002

### 3.3 Communication Complaints

A communication complaint concerns missing, unclear, inconsistent or incorrect information provided to a customer.

The investigation must review relevant customer communications and identify where the communication process failed.

**Rule ID:** COM-CLASS-003

### 3.4 Repeat Complaints

A repeat complaint occurs when a customer raises the same or substantially similar issue after an earlier complaint was closed or when the original problem remains unresolved.

The new complaint must be linked to the previous complaint reference where possible.

**Rule ID:** COM-CLASS-004

### 3.5 Complaint About a Previous Resolution

A customer may challenge a complaint outcome when they believe the investigation was incomplete or the proposed resolution did not address the original issue.

Such complaints must be reviewed by an authorized person who was not solely responsible for the original decision, where operationally feasible.

**Rule ID:** COM-CLASS-005

## 4. Complaint Registration

### 4.1 Required Information

A complaint should include:

- Customer reference.
- Date of complaint.
- Complaint category.
- Description of the issue.
- Relevant transaction or service request reference, where applicable.
- Previous complaint reference, if available.
- Preferred communication method.

**Rule ID:** COM-REG-001

### 4.2 Complaint Reference

Every registered complaint must receive a unique complaint reference number.

The reference must be used in subsequent communications and investigation records.

**Rule ID:** COM-REG-002

### 4.3 Initial Acknowledgement

For this synthetic policy, a complete complaint must be acknowledged within one business day after registration.

The acknowledgement must include:

- Complaint reference number.
- Summary of the reported issue.
- Expected next update.
- Available communication channel.

**Rule ID:** COM-REG-003

### 4.4 Incomplete Complaints

If essential information is missing, the complaints team must request the information needed to investigate the issue.

The complaint must remain recorded while awaiting additional information.

**Rule ID:** COM-REG-004

## 5. Complaint Investigation

### 5.1 Initial Assessment

The complaints team must review the complaint category, customer description, previous interactions and relevant service records.

**Rule ID:** COM-INV-001

### 5.2 Ownership Assignment

Each complaint must be assigned to an appropriate team based on its classification.

Examples:

| Complaint Category | Responsible Team |
|---|---|
| Account servicing | Customer Operations |
| Card service delay | Card Operations |
| Transfer service concern | Payments Operations |
| Employee conduct | Customer Experience |
| Fee-related complaint | Banking Finance |
| Suspected fraud | Fraud Operations |

**Rule ID:** COM-INV-002

### 5.3 Investigation Evidence

The investigation must retain relevant evidence, including:

- Customer communications.
- Service request history.
- Transaction references, where applicable.
- Operational records.
- Previous complaint outcomes.
- Actions performed by responsible teams.

**Rule ID:** COM-INV-003

### 5.4 Investigation Findings

The investigator must document:

- What happened.
- Why the issue occurred.
- Whether a process failure was identified.
- Actions taken to resolve the issue.
- Whether additional escalation is required.

**Rule ID:** COM-INV-004

## 6. Complaint Resolution

### 6.1 Resolution Requirements

A complaint may be closed only after the investigation outcome and resolution actions have been documented.

The resolution must address the customer's reported concern.

**Rule ID:** COM-RES-001

### 6.2 Resolution Outcomes

Each complaint must be assigned one of the following outcomes:

1. Complaint upheld.
2. Complaint partially upheld.
3. Complaint not upheld.
4. Service issue corrected.
5. Additional investigation required.
6. Escalated to a specialist team.

**Rule ID:** COM-RES-002

### 6.3 Customer Communication

The customer must receive an explanation of the investigation outcome and any corrective action taken.

Where a complaint is not upheld, the response must provide a clear explanation based on the available evidence.

**Rule ID:** COM-RES-003

### 6.4 Complaint Closure

A complaint may be marked closed after:

- The investigation is completed.
- The outcome is documented.
- Required corrective actions are recorded.
- The customer has been informed of the outcome.

**Rule ID:** COM-RES-004

## 7. Complaint Escalation

### 7.1 Escalation Conditions

A complaint must be considered for escalation when:

- The expected resolution timeline is exceeded.
- The customer raises repeated complaints about the same issue.
- The issue affects multiple customers.
- A potential regulatory or compliance concern is identified.
- The complaint involves suspected fraud or unauthorized activity.
- The customer challenges a previous investigation outcome.
- The issue requires authority beyond the assigned team's responsibility.

**Rule ID:** COM-ESC-001

### 7.2 Escalation Ownership

Complaints may be escalated to:

- Customer Complaints Manager.
- Banking Operations Manager.
- Compliance Department.
- Fraud Operations.
- Relevant specialist department.

The escalation record must include the complaint reference, reason, investigation history and actions already taken.

**Rule ID:** COM-ESC-002

### 7.3 Regulatory or Compliance Concerns

Potential regulatory or compliance concerns must be referred to the appropriate authorized team.

Customer-facing AI assistants must not independently determine regulatory liability or promise a regulatory outcome.

**Rule ID:** COM-ESC-003

## 8. Complaint Processing Timelines

The following are synthetic demonstration targets:

| Activity | Target Processing Time |
|---|---|
| Complaint acknowledgement | One business day |
| Initial complaint assessment | Two business days |
| First progress update | Three business days |
| Standard complaint resolution | Five business days |
| Escalated complaint review | Three business days after escalation |
| Customer notification after final decision | One business day |

Cases requiring external evidence, specialist review or additional customer information may take longer.

These targets are not guaranteed resolution times.

**Rule ID:** COM-TIME-001

## 9. Repeat Complaint Management

### 9.1 Previous Complaint Review

When a repeat complaint is identified, the investigator must review the previous complaint reference and its resolution history.

**Rule ID:** COM-REPEAT-001

### 9.2 Root Cause Identification

Repeated complaints involving the same operational issue must be reviewed for potential process or system failures.

**Rule ID:** COM-REPEAT-002

### 9.3 Corrective Actions

Where a recurring issue is confirmed, the responsible department must document corrective actions and assign an owner.

**Rule ID:** COM-REPEAT-003

## 10. Customer Communication Standards

1. Communication must be clear, professional and factual.
2. Customers must receive a complaint reference number.
3. Expected timelines must be described as targets, not guarantees.
4. Customers must be informed when additional information is required.
5. Unresolved complaints must not be represented as completed.
6. Employees must not promise refunds, fee waivers or financial compensation without authorization.

**Rule ID:** COM-COMM-001

## 11. General Controls

1. Every complaint must be recorded and traceable.
2. Complaint categories must be assigned consistently.
3. Investigation evidence must be retained.
4. Complaint ownership must be clearly assigned.
5. Escalation decisions must be documented.
6. Repeat complaints must be linked where possible.
7. Customer information must be handled according to applicable access and privacy controls.
8. AI assistants must not independently close complaints or approve compensation.

**Rule ID:** COM-GEN-001

## 12. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Complaint | Expression of dissatisfaction concerning a banking service or experience |
| Complaint reference | Unique identifier assigned to a registered complaint |
| Escalation | Referral of an issue to a higher authority or specialist team |
| Complaint upheld | Investigation confirms that the complaint is supported by evidence |
| Complaint partially upheld | Investigation confirms only part of the reported concern |
| Root cause | Underlying reason that caused the reported issue |
| Corrective action | Action taken to address an identified problem |
