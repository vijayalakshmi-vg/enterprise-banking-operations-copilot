## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the procedures and requirements for updating customer personal information maintained by the bank.

It establishes eligibility criteria, identity verification requirements, supporting documentation, processing timelines, exceptions and escalation procedures.

The objective is to ensure customer information is updated accurately, securely and consistently.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to individual customers holding active personal banking accounts.

It covers:

- Residential address updates.
- Registered phone number updates.
- Registered email address updates.
- Identity verification.
- Supporting document requirements.
- Processing timelines.
- Exceptions and escalation procedures.

Business banking accounts are outside the scope of this demonstration policy.

## 3. Address Update Policy

### 3.1 Eligibility

Customers with active personal banking accounts may request changes to their registered residential address.

The customer must successfully complete identity verification before the address change can be processed.

**Rule ID:** CIM-ADDR-001

### 3.2 Required Documents

Customers must submit one approved proof-of-address document.

The accepted documents for this synthetic policy are:

- Utility bill.
- Government-issued address certificate.
- Bank statement issued by another financial institution.

The submitted document must be dated within the previous 60 calendar days.

**Rule ID:** CIM-ADDR-002

### 3.3 Processing Timeline

Once the required documents are received and successfully verified, the standard processing time is two business days.

Incomplete, unclear or inconsistent documents may delay processing.

**Rule ID:** CIM-ADDR-003

### 3.4 Exceptions

The following exceptions apply to address updates:

1. Accounts under an unresolved security restriction cannot process address updates.
2. Recently opened accounts may require additional identity verification.
3. Documents older than 60 calendar days must be rejected under the standard procedure.
4. Incomplete verification requests must remain pending.

**Rule ID:** CIM-ADDR-004

## 4. Phone Number Update Policy

### 4.1 Eligibility

Customers with active personal banking accounts may request changes to their registered phone number.

Identity verification through an approved procedure is mandatory.

**Rule ID:** CIM-PHONE-001

### 4.2 Verification Requirements

The customer must verify their identity using the existing registered contact method or an approved alternative verification procedure.

A phone number update must not be completed solely on the basis of an unverified request.

**Rule ID:** CIM-PHONE-002

### 4.3 Processing Timeline

A successfully verified phone number update should be completed within one business day.

Requests requiring manual identity verification may take longer.

**Rule ID:** CIM-PHONE-003

### 4.4 Exceptions

If the customer cannot access the existing registered phone number, the request must be referred for an alternative identity verification procedure.

The new number must not be activated until verification is completed.

**Rule ID:** CIM-PHONE-004

## 5. Email Address Update Policy

### 5.1 Eligibility

Customers may request an update to their registered email address after successfully completing identity verification.

**Rule ID:** CIM-EMAIL-001

### 5.2 Verification Requirements

The new email address must pass the synthetic demonstration verification procedure.

A confirmation notification should be sent to the existing registered email address when available.

**Rule ID:** CIM-EMAIL-002

### 5.3 Processing Timeline

The standard processing time is one business day after successful verification.

**Rule ID:** CIM-EMAIL-003

### 5.4 Exceptions

If the existing email address is inaccessible, the customer must complete an approved alternative verification procedure.

Unverified email changes must remain pending.

**Rule ID:** CIM-EMAIL-004

## 6. General Restrictions

The following restrictions apply to all personal information updates:

1. Requests with incomplete identity verification must remain pending.
2. Restricted accounts must be referred for authorized review.
3. Customer information must not be disclosed to unauthorized individuals.
4. Employees must not bypass identity verification requirements.
5. Every completed update must have an auditable record.
6. Conflicting customer information must be investigated before an update is approved.

**Rule ID:** CIM-GEN-001

## 7. Escalation Procedure

Requests must be escalated to the Customer Operations review team when:

- Identity verification repeatedly fails.
- Submitted documents appear inconsistent.
- An account has an unresolved security restriction.
- The customer disputes a rejected information-update request.
- An alternative verification procedure is required.

The review team must record the reason for escalation, the investigation outcome and the final resolution.

**Rule ID:** CIM-ESC-001

## 8. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Active account | An account currently eligible for normal operations |
| Restricted account | An account subject to an operational or security restriction |
| Proof of address | An approved document supporting a customer's residential address |
| Business day | A working day excluding weekends and designated bank holidays |
| Identity verification | A procedure used to establish the identity of a customer |
| Pending request | A request awaiting additional information or verification |
