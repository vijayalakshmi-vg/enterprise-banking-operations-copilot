# Account Opening and KYC Policy

**Document ID:** POL-KYC-005  
**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Department:** Customer Onboarding and Compliance  
**Document Owner:** KYC Operations Manager  
**Status:** Synthetic Demonstration Policy  
**Effective Date:** 01 November 2026  
**Review Status:** Not an official banking policy

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the requirements and procedures for opening new personal banking accounts and completing Know Your Customer (KYC) verification.

It establishes customer eligibility, documentation requirements, identity verification, risk assessment, application review and account activation procedures.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to individuals applying for personal banking accounts through the synthetic banking platform.

It covers:

- Customer eligibility.
- Account opening applications.
- Identity document requirements.
- Address verification.
- KYC verification.
- Customer risk assessment.
- Application approval and rejection.
- Account activation.
- Exceptions and escalation.

Business banking and corporate customer onboarding are outside the scope of this demonstration policy.

## 3. Customer Eligibility

### 3.1 Basic Eligibility

Applicants must meet the following synthetic demonstration requirements:

1. The applicant must be at least 18 years old.
2. The applicant must provide valid identity information.
3. The applicant must provide the required supporting documents.
4. The applicant must successfully complete the applicable verification procedures.

**Rule ID:** KYC-ELIG-001

### 3.2 Ineligible Applications

Applications must not proceed to account activation when mandatory eligibility requirements are not satisfied.

Incomplete applications may remain pending while additional information is requested.

**Rule ID:** KYC-ELIG-002

## 4. Account Opening Application

### 4.1 Required Information

Applicants must provide:

- Full legal name.
- Date of birth.
- Residential address.
- Registered phone number.
- Email address.
- Required identity document information.

**Rule ID:** KYC-APP-001

### 4.2 Application Submission

The application must be submitted through an approved banking channel.

Each application must receive a unique application reference number.

**Rule ID:** KYC-APP-002

### 4.3 Incomplete Applications

Applications containing missing mandatory information must be classified as incomplete.

The applicant must be notified about the missing information required to continue processing.

**Rule ID:** KYC-APP-003

## 5. Identity Document Verification

### 5.1 Accepted Documents

For this synthetic policy, accepted identity documents include:

- Government-issued identity card.
- Valid passport.
- Government-issued driving licence.

The document must contain sufficient information to establish the applicant's identity.

**Rule ID:** KYC-ID-001

### 5.2 Document Validity

Submitted identity documents must be valid and must not have expired.

Documents with unreadable or inconsistent information must be referred for manual review.

**Rule ID:** KYC-ID-002

### 5.3 Identity Verification Failure

If automated identity verification fails, the application must be referred for an alternative verification procedure.

An account must not be activated solely because the applicant submits an additional document without successful verification.

**Rule ID:** KYC-ID-003

## 6. Address Verification

### 6.1 Proof of Address

Applicants must provide an approved proof-of-address document.

For this synthetic policy, acceptable documents include:

- Utility bill.
- Government-issued address certificate.
- Bank statement from another financial institution.

**Rule ID:** KYC-ADDR-001

### 6.2 Document Recency

Proof-of-address documents must be dated within the previous 90 calendar days.

Documents outside this period must be rejected under the standard procedure.

**Rule ID:** KYC-ADDR-002

### 6.3 Address Mismatch

If the address entered in the application differs from the submitted proof-of-address document, the application must be referred for additional review.

**Rule ID:** KYC-ADDR-003

## 7. KYC Verification Process

### 7.1 Verification Requirements

All applicants must complete the applicable identity and document verification procedures before account approval.

**Rule ID:** KYC-VER-001

### 7.2 Verification Status

For this synthetic policy, verification statuses are:

- Not Started.
- In Progress.
- Verified.
- Failed.
- Pending Manual Review.

**Rule ID:** KYC-VER-002

### 7.3 Manual Verification

Applications requiring manual verification must be reviewed by authorized KYC Operations personnel.

The reviewer must document the verification outcome and supporting reasons.

**Rule ID:** KYC-VER-003

## 8. Customer Risk Assessment

### 8.1 Risk Classification

For this synthetic demonstration, customer applications may be classified as:

- Low Risk.
- Medium Risk.
- High Risk.

The classification must be based on the approved assessment criteria.

**Rule ID:** KYC-RISK-001

### 8.2 Additional Review

Applications classified as high risk must undergo additional review by authorized compliance personnel.

**Rule ID:** KYC-RISK-002

### 8.3 Unresolved Risk Concerns

Applications with unresolved risk concerns must not proceed to final approval until the required review is completed.

**Rule ID:** KYC-RISK-003

## 9. Application Approval and Rejection

### 9.1 Approval Requirements

An application may be approved when:

1. Mandatory application information is complete.
2. Required documents are valid.
3. Identity verification is successful.
4. Applicable KYC checks are completed.
5. Required reviews and authorizations are obtained.

**Rule ID:** KYC-APPROVE-001

### 9.2 Application Rejection

An application may be rejected when mandatory eligibility requirements are not met or verification cannot be completed successfully under the applicable procedure.

The rejection reason must be recorded.

**Rule ID:** KYC-APPROVE-002

### 9.3 Pending Applications

Applications awaiting additional documents, verification or authorized review must remain pending.

Pending applications must not be treated as approved.

**Rule ID:** KYC-APPROVE-003

## 10. Account Activation

### 10.1 Activation Eligibility

An account may be activated only after the application has received final approval.

**Rule ID:** KYC-ACT-001

### 10.2 Activation Confirmation

After successful activation, the system must record the account reference and activation status.

The applicant should receive an activation confirmation notification.

**Rule ID:** KYC-ACT-002

### 10.3 Activation Restrictions

Accounts with incomplete KYC verification or unresolved mandatory compliance reviews must not be activated.

**Rule ID:** KYC-ACT-003

## 11. Processing Timelines

For this synthetic policy, the following processing targets apply:

| Activity | Target Processing Time |
|---|---|
| Initial application review | One business day |
| Automated document verification | Same business day |
| Manual verification | Three business days |
| Additional document review | Two business days |

These are demonstration targets, not guaranteed completion times.

Applications requiring additional investigation may take longer.

**Rule ID:** KYC-TIME-001

## 12. Exceptions and Escalation

Applications must be escalated to the KYC Operations Manager when:

- Identity verification repeatedly fails.
- Documents contain inconsistent information.
- The applicant's identity cannot be established.
- High-risk classification requires additional authorization.
- Mandatory compliance checks remain unresolved.
- An applicant disputes an application rejection.

The escalation record must contain the application reference, reason for escalation and investigation outcome.

**Rule ID:** KYC-ESC-001

## 13. General Controls

1. Customer identity documents must only be accessed by authorized personnel.
2. Sensitive customer information must not be disclosed to unauthorized individuals.
3. KYC verification requirements must not be bypassed.
4. Application approval decisions must be auditable.
5. Original submitted documents must be retained according to the applicable document retention procedure.
6. Automated verification results must not replace mandatory human review where manual review is required.

**Rule ID:** KYC-GEN-001
